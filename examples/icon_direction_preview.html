04 / Design decisions

One family. Uniform outline construction, optical weight and corners.

Semantic color. Gold for folders, blue for PYNIX code, neutral files and controls.

Retina-native. Vector scaling without storing competing 1×/2× PNG sets.

License. Third-party glyphs remain identifiable and license attribution must be retained in the final asset bundle.
